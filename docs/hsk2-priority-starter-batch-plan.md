# DuongDuong 学中文 — HSK2 Priority Starter Batch Plan

_Last updated: 2026-05-07_

## 1. Purpose
This file defines the first HSK2 starter content direction after HSK1 is stable.

The goal is not full HSK2 coverage immediately.
The goal is to create a practical HSK2 layer that supports daily communication.

---

## 2. Starter batch recommendation
### Recommended size
50 words

### Why 50 first
- enough to test HSK2 lesson expansion
- enough to test level selector later
- small enough for clean QA
- helps validate HSK2 lesson structure before scaling to 500 words

---

## 3. Topic buckets
### Bucket A — Everyday Interaction
- daily small talk
- family and friends
- common responses

### Bucket B — Practical Life
- shopping
- prices
- ordering food
- transportation

### Bucket C — Schedule & Plans
- time
- frequency
- routine
- simple plans

### Bucket D — Preference & Feelings
- likes
- dislikes
- reactions
- comparisons

---

## 4. Selection rule
Pick words that:
- are clearly above HSK1
- are still highly useful in real daily communication
- help form longer but still practical sentences
- appear often in learner materials

---

## 5. Product note
HSK2 starter content should feel like:
- more fluid everyday life Chinese
- not abstract Chinese yet
- still practical, useful, and repeatable in app review mode
