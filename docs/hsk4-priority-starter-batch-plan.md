# DuongDuong 学中文 — HSK4 Priority Starter Batch Plan

_Last updated: 2026-05-07_

## 1. Purpose
This file defines the first HSK4 starter batch.

The goal is to introduce:
- work and social vocabulary
- emotions and psychology
- opinion / decision language
- more precise communication

---

## 2. Starter batch recommendation
### Recommended size
60 words

### Why slightly more than HSK2/HSK3
HSK4 starts to require richer clusters:
- collocations
- emotional nuance
- topic-specific words

A 60-word starter batch gives enough density to test those patterns.

---

## 3. Topic buckets
### Bucket A — Work & Productivity
- responsibility
- efficiency
- communication
- process

### Bucket B — Emotion & Psychology
- stress
- confidence
- disappointment
- self-reflection

### Bucket C — Discussion & Opinion
- agree / disagree
- influence
- reason
- effect

### Bucket D — Social & Daily Commentary
- lifestyle
- learning growth
- society basics

---

## 4. Selection rule
Choose words that:
- show the jump from HSK3 to HSK4 clearly
- are useful in real life and exam use
- support collocation and near-synonym practice later

---

## 5. Product note
HSK4 is the level where review quality becomes much more important than just adding more words.
