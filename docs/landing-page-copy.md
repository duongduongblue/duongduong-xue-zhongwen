## FAQ
### Is DuongDuong 学中文 for beginners?
Yes. The current MVP starts with HSK1 vocabulary and beginner-friendly study flow.

### Do I need an account?
Not for the MVP. Progress can work locally first.

### Is this a full HSK app already?
Not yet. The current version focuses on a strong HSK1-based vocabulary experience.
