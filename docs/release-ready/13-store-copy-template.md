## Full description
DuongDuong 学中文 is a mobile-first Chinese learning app designed for learners who want a calm, simple, and consistent study experience.

The app focuses on:
- small vocabulary lessons
- flashcard review
- short quizzes
- daily progress and streak motivation

It is especially suitable for:
- beginners
- self-learners
- people who prefer short daily study sessions

Current MVP focus:
- HSK1 vocabulary
- lesson-based learning
- flashcard review
- quick quiz practice
- progress tracking
